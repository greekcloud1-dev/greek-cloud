Accessibility statement

Accessibility statement

We are committed to making this site usable by everyone, including people with disabilities, in line with the Israeli standard IS 5568 and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Act.

Draft — not for publication until completed This page describes the technical state of the site accurately, but it still lacks the legal entity details and contact routes, and it has not been reviewed by a certified accessibility expert. The fields marked below must be filled in before this goes live.

01

Level of accessibility

This site was built to meet IS 5568, which is anchored to WCAG 2.0 level AA. Accessibility was part of the build from the start rather than retrofitted afterwards.

We want to be precise about one thing: the accessibility menu does not, by itself, make this site accessible. It is a comfort tool that lets you adapt the display to your preferences. The accessibility itself is built into the code — in the semantic structure, the contrast, keyboard navigation and screen reader support.

02

What is accessible

Structure and navigation

  • A "skip to main content" link as the first element on every page, which genuinely moves focus
  • Semantic landmarks: header, nav, main, footer
  • A single h1 per page and a continuous heading hierarchy
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus state on every interactive element
  • lang and dir declared correctly on every page, in both languages

Colour and text

  • Contrast of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text — checked automatically on every page
  • Light and dark display modes, both meeting the same contrast requirement
  • Text can be enlarged to 150% without loss of content or function
  • Information is never conveyed by colour alone

Forms

  • A visible, associated label on every field
  • Error messages next to the field itself, not only at the top of the form
  • aria-invalid marking and an aria-live region announcing status

Privacy and cookies

  • The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies and loads no third-party scripts
  • A short privacy notice is shown on a first visit and dismissed with one button
  • Display preferences are stored in your own browser only and are never sent to a server

Motion

  • prefers-reduced-motion honoured at system level
  • A separate reduce-motion preference in the accessibility menu
  • No flashing, auto-moving or auto-rotating content

03

The accessibility menu

There is a menu button at the top of every page. You can also open it with Alt + A from anywhere on the site, and close it with Esc.

The menu also holds full navigation to every page on the site, plus:

  • Theme — system, light or dark
  • Language — Hebrew or English
  • Text size — normal, 110%, 125% or 150%
  • Line spacing — normal, wide or widest
  • Contrast — normal, high, inverted or greyscale
  • Underline links — so links are identifiable without relying on colour
  • Simpler font — replaces the hand-drawn display face with a readable system font
  • Outline headings — marks every heading with a dashed outline
  • Large cursor
  • Reduce motion
  • Reset — returns everything to its default

In inverted and greyscale contrast modes the menu itself is excluded from the filter — otherwise you could not read the one control needed to switch them back off.

What the panel does not do It does not alter page content, generate alternative text for images, or rewrite ARIA markup. It only applies display preferences that you choose. Your preferences are stored in your own browser and reapplied automatically on your next visit.

04

Known accessibility limitations

We would rather set out what is not yet finished than claim full accessibility:

  • No review by a certified service-accessibility expert has been carried out yet. The testing done so far is automated and development-level, and is not a substitute for a professional opinion.
  • No manual screen reader testing has been carried out yet (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) with real users. We intend to complete this.
  • The heading font on this site is a hand-drawn face. It was chosen for design reasons, and in some letterforms it can be less legible. That is why the menu offers a "simpler heading font" option, and why narrow screens switch away from it automatically.
  • The form is not yet connected to an intake system. Until it is, submission is blocked and the message explaining that is shown as text and announced to screen readers.

If you encounter an accessibility problem that is not listed here, we would genuinely like to know. Reports of this kind are treated as a priority.

05

Accessibility contact

To complete before publication
  • Name of the accessibility coordinator — required for a public body or an employer with 25 or more employees. Otherwise a dedicated contact route is sufficient.
  • A dedicated email address for accessibility enquiries
  • A telephone number
  • Legal entity name and registration number
  • Date of the most recent review by a certified accessibility expert

Accessibility enquiries are handled by the contact to be listed here. If an enquiry goes unanswered, you may contact the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities at the Israeli Ministry of Justice.
