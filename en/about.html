01

What GreekCloud actually does

We coordinate a visitor's request to a Greek-licensed physician, remotely, before the flight. That is the whole service.

In practice: you complete a questionnaire, we check that the request is complete and clear and pass it to the physician, the physician reviews and decides independently, and if they approve, the digital prescription reaches you in the name on your passport. From there you visit a licensed pharmacy in Greece. The full explanation of the remote process.

Our job is to remove friction: language, documents, timing, and knowing what to expect at the pharmacy counter. We decide nothing medical.

02

What we are not

This section matters more than the last one, because every expensive misunderstanding lives here.

  • We are not doctors and we do not give medical advice. We have no view on what treatment suits you.
  • We are not a pharmacy and we do not sell, supply or transport cannabis products. No product passes through us.
  • We do not issue prescriptions. The physician issues them, and only the physician.
  • We do not influence the physician's decision. We cannot promote, accelerate or bias an approval.
  • We are not lawyers. Content on this site is general information, not legal advice.
  • We do not help anyone take cannabis out of Greece. It is prohibited, and we say so on every relevant page.
A line we do not cross If anyone offers to help you fly with cannabis, or to notarise a foreign licence so you are "covered", or to bring home what you bought in Greece — that is not us, and it does not work. The detail is on the flying page.

03

Why the service exists

Almost everything written about medical cannabis in Greece answers one question: how to bring in what you already have. Guides explain embassy approvals, notarised translations, border-police forms.

That question is relevant to a minority. Most people searching hold no licence at all, so there is nothing to translate and nothing to bring. What does exist for them is an entirely different route: a prescription from a Greek-licensed physician, issued and dispensed in Greece. Almost nobody writes about that route.

GreekCloud was built to close that gap — with the people actually authorised to decide, and without anyone having to rely on a guide written in 2021.

04

How we work

What happens to your information

The questionnaire collects what the physician needs in order to decide, and nothing more. It is stored privately and passed to the physician for the review only. The privacy policy sets out what is collected, for how long, and what rights you have.

When the fee is charged

The service fee is settled after you send the questionnaire, on WhatsApp, before the physician reviews the request. If the physician does not approve, the full amount is refunded with no cancellation charge. Cancellations and refunds.

How we write the content

Every legal claim on the site carries a source you can check, and every page is dated. When the regulation changes, and in Greece it does — we update the page and say what changed and when.

05

What we do not promise

  • We do not promise approval. The physician may decline, and that happens.
  • We do not promise a specific product. Pharmacy availability is not ours to control.
  • We do not promise a pharmacy price. It is set there, and is not part of the service fee.
  • We do not promise an absolute timeline. Our published times are targets, and they depend on you too.

If that list reads like legal hedging, fair enough, but it was written from the other side. We would rather lose a request than sell an expectation we cannot meet.

06

Who is responsible for the content

Content on this site is written and maintained by the GreekCloud team.

Medical requests are assessed by a Greek-licensed physician. GreekCloud does not employ the physician as a content contributor and does not present their clinical opinion as marketing.
