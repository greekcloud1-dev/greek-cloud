Mainland · Three peninsulas

Medical cannabis in Halkidiki for visitors

Halkidiki is not an island and has no airport of its own. You arrive through Thessaloniki and drive out to one of three peninsulas, and the distance is what decides your planning, including where it makes sense to redeem a prescription.

AirportThessaloniki (SKG), ~13 km from the city
Getting thereWell connected across Europe, year-round
Prescription validity30 days, all of Greece

Current as of 11 September 2026. General information, not medical advice.

01 · Geography

Three peninsulas, three different realities

"Halkidiki" covers three long fingers of land, and they are not interchangeable:

  • Kassandra: the closest and busiest, with the densest run of resorts and the shortest drive.
  • Sithonia: quieter and more spread out, with longer distances between villages and a thinner services network.
  • Athos: the eastern peninsula, most of which is the autonomous monastic community of Mount Athos and not a tourist destination at all.

Ouranoupoli, at the edge of the Athos peninsula, is where secular Halkidiki ends. It is also the furthest point from the airport that a visitor would realistically stay.

Mount Athos is not part of any plan Mount Athos is a self-governing monastic community. Entry requires a permit, women are not admitted, and nothing about redeeming a prescription there is possible. If your itinerary mentions Athos, it means Ouranoupoli and the boat trips along the coast, not the monastic territory itself.

02 · Distances

How long the drive actually takes

This is the only practical question that really matters here. From Thessaloniki airport:

DestinationRoughlyDrive
Kassandra (northern end)~77 kmabout an hour
Nikiti / Neos Marmaras (northern Sithonia)—about 1¼ hours
Southern Sithonia · Ouranoupoli—2 hours or more

Those are normal-conditions figures. In peak season the coastal roads slow considerably, and the last stretch on Sithonia is winding rather than fast.

The planning consequence is simple: redeem in Thessaloniki before you drive out. The city has a full year-round pharmacy network; the resort strips do not. If a pharmacy is out of stock once you are on a peninsula, the fallback is the same drive again.

03 · Pharmacies

What the pharmacy situation looks like

In the larger Halkidiki towns there are working pharmacies year-round. In the seasonal resort strips the picture changes with the calendar: between roughly November and March some businesses close and opening hours contract.

Greek pharmacy hours are set regionally, not nationally, with mandatory opening Monday to Friday only. In summer the district pharmaceutical association publishes extended hours for the main towns. The duty rota, which is legally binding and covers evenings and weekends, is posted on the front of every closed pharmacy and on the regional association's site. The full rules are on the redemption page.

Stock is the real variable A pharmacy being open is not the same as it holding the product, and no list of dispensing pharmacies is published by EOF or the Health Ministry. Calling ahead is worth the two minutes — more so here than in a city.

04 · Season

The long summer, and the quiet winter

Halkidiki's season runs long, the coast stays pleasant well into autumn, but it does end. Thessaloniki works all year as a real city rather than a resort, so the contrast between the city and the peninsulas widens in winter.

If you are visiting outside the season, treat the city as the place where everything gets done, and the peninsula as somewhere you go afterwards.

Do not take it home The prescription and the product are valid inside Greece only. Carrying either across a border is a separate legal question with a different answer — see flying with cannabis and penalties in Greece.

05 · Questions

Questions about Halkidiki

Does Halkidiki have its own airport?
No. Halkidiki is served by Thessaloniki "Macedonia" Airport (SKG), roughly 13 km south-east of the city centre. From there it is a drive to whichever peninsula you are staying on.
How long is the drive from Thessaloniki airport to Halkidiki?
It depends which peninsula. Kassandra is roughly 77 km and about an hour. Northern Sithonia, around Nikiti and Neos Marmaras, is about an hour and a quarter. Southern Sithonia and Ouranoupoli are two hours or more.
Where should I redeem the prescription if I am staying in Halkidiki?
Thessaloniki, before you drive out, is usually the easier choice. The city has a full year-round pharmacy network, while the resort strips are thinner and partly seasonal, and once you are on a peninsula, a second attempt means the drive again.
Can I collect a prescription on Mount Athos?
No. Mount Athos is an autonomous monastic community, not a tourist destination. Entry requires a permit, women are not admitted at all, and it should not feature in any plan for redeeming a prescription.
What changes outside the summer season in Halkidiki?
In the seasonal resort strips some businesses close and pharmacy hours contract between roughly November and March. Thessaloniki and the larger Halkidiki towns operate year-round, which is another reason to redeem in the city.
