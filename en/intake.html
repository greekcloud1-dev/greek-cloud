Eligibility check

A few questions about your health and your passport details. A Greek-licensed physician will review the request and decide independently. Filling this in commits you to nothing.

Time to completeAbout 3 minutes
What you needPassport number and a selfie
If not approvedFull refund
Plan

The only difference is the physician's response time. You can change this later.

Personal details

These must match your passport exactly. The prescription is issued in your passport name, and the pharmacy checks it against the document.

For example: ISRAEL ISRAELI

As printed in the passport, no spaces

The service is for adults aged 18 and over

Your health

Write freely and in your own words. The physician reads this — the more specific you are, the better grounded their assessment.

Not a requirement and not valid in Greece — asked only to inform the assessment

Identity verification

One selfie, to verify against the passport details you entered. We do not ask for a scan of the passport itself.

Face only, in good light. It does not need to be a formal photo.

Optional

Confirmations
Final declaration

This is the most important part of the form. The prescription is issued from what you entered here, and it cannot be corrected once issued.

Before you send Take out your passport and compare letter by letter: given name, surname and number. This one check prevents most of the problems we see.

What happens next

After you submit

  • The request is passed to a Greek-licensed physician for review.
  • The physician may ask for clarification before deciding.
  • Approved — a digital prescription in your passport name, by email.
  • Not approved — the service fee is refunded in full.
About the information you send Your details and selfie are used solely to pass the request to the physician and have it reviewed, and are stored encrypted. We do not ask for and do not store a scan of your passport. Full details in the privacy policy.
