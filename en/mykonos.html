Medical Cannabis in Mykonos for visitors

Mykonos is an intense nightlife destination, and precisely because of that it is worth being exact about what is permitted: the prescription is valid, but it is not a licence to use anywhere.

The place

What to know about Mykonos

  • Chora, the port town, concentrates the services
  • A small island — distances measured in minutes
  • An international crowd, English everywhere
  • Good connections to Santorini and Athens

Pharmacies are in Chora. Outside town, in the beach villages and club areas, there is no real infrastructure. At peak season the island is extremely crowded and nothing should be left to the last moment.

Local tipA point worth emphasising here in particular: use is intended for private accommodation. Mykonos is a town of clubs and beaches, and the prescription does not cover use in public space, not on the beach, not in a club, not in the street.

Seasonality

When to come, and what changes

Mykonos runs at full intensity between May and October and quietens considerably in winter. Direct flights from Tel Aviv and much of Europe run in season, roughly May to October; the rest of the year the route goes through Athens. In season the island is crowded, prices are high, and traffic between Chora and the beaches is slow. Off-season Chora keeps functioning but most nightlife closes.

Planning your timing

Mykonos is a destination people visit briefly and at speed, the opposite of what running a medical process on location requires. A prescription arranged in advance is not a convenience here; it is the practical condition.

The process

Arriving with a prescription in hand

The process is the same everywhere in Greece, and the prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in the country, not only in Mykonos. You fill in a questionnaire, a Greek-licensed physician reviews it remotely, before you fly, and decides independently. If approved, you receive a digital prescription in your passport name. Full details in the guide.

What you buy in Greece stays in Greece The prescription and products are valid inside Greece only. Taking cannabis across a border is a criminal offence in most destinations, and a Greek prescription will not protect you at arrival. Details on the flying page.

At the pharmacy

Redeeming in Mykonos

Redemption itself is short: you walk into a licensed pharmacy, show your passport, and the pharmacist retrieves the digital prescription from the system. This is the only step in the whole process that requires you to be physically in Greece. How redemption works without a Greek social security number.

Questions

What people ask us

Can I use it at the beach or in a club?
No. This matters especially in Mykonos: the prescription permits possession and use in a medical context, in private accommodation. It does not cover public space — beach, club, bar or street. Enforcement in those places exists.
I'm going from Mykonos to Santorini. Does the prescription still apply?
Yes. The prescription is valid throughout Greece, and moving between islands is not a border crossing. Carry the prescription and your passport on the ferry as well.
Where is the pharmacy on Mykonos?
In Chora, the port town. There is no real infrastructure in the beach villages or the club areas. The island is small and distances are minutes, but traffic between Chora and the beaches is slow in season.
Is anything open on Mykonos in winter?
Chora keeps working, but most of the nightlife closes. Direct flights run roughly May to October; the rest of the year the route goes through Athens.
