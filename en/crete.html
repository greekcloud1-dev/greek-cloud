The place

What to know about Crete

  • The only Greek island with two active international airports
  • Real cities with full infrastructure, not just resort villages
  • Pharmacies open year-round, including outside the season
  • Direct summer flights from much of Europe

Most licensed pharmacies are concentrated in Heraklion, Chania and Rethymno. If you are staying at a resort in the south or east of the island, the distance to a dispensing pharmacy can be significant — worth checking before you set out, not after.

Local tipCrete is the destination where arranging the prescription before you land pays off most. Not because of availability, but because of distance: a round trip to a pharmacy from the south can cost you the better part of a day.

Seasonality

When to come, and what changes

Crete has the longest season of any Greek island. Temperatures stay pleasant from April through November, and in winter the island keeps functioning — mainly in the larger towns. Resort villages in the south and east shrink dramatically between November and March, but Heraklion, Chania and Rethymno operate all year.

Planning your timing

Because the island is long and the roads are slow, timing matters more here than almost anywhere else in Greece. Arriving with the prescription already issued removes the single biggest logistical cost of the trip.

The process

Arriving with a prescription in hand

The process is the same everywhere in Greece, and the prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in the country. You fill in a questionnaire, a Greek-licensed physician reviews it and decides independently, and if approved you receive a digital prescription in your passport name.

You can start before you fly. Full details in the guide, including response times by plan and what happens if the physician does not approve.

What you buy in Greece stays in Greece The prescription and products are valid inside Greece only. Taking cannabis across a border is a criminal offence in most destinations, and a Greek prescription will not protect you at arrival. Details on the flying page.

At the pharmacy

Redeeming in Crete

Redemption itself is short: you walk into a licensed pharmacy, show your passport, and the pharmacist retrieves the digital prescription from the system. This is the only step in the whole process that requires you to be physically in Greece. How redemption works without a Greek social security number.

Questions

What people ask us

I'm staying in Elounda. Where is the nearest pharmacy?
Elounda is in the east, so you would likely go to Agios Nikolaos or Heraklion. We do not keep a live address list and will not invent one — on the VIP plan we help locate a dispensing pharmacy near your accommodation, based on a real-time check.
I land in Chania but I'm driving to Heraklion. Does it matter where I redeem?
No. The prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in Greece. Redeem wherever is convenient, at any point in your trip.
I am staying in the south or the east. How long will this take?
Most licensed pharmacies are concentrated in Heraklion, Chania and Rethymno. From a resort village in the south or east the drive can reach an hour and a half each way. Check before you set off, not after.
Do pharmacies in Crete open in winter?
In the cities, yes. Heraklion, Chania and Rethymno operate all year. Resort villages in the south and east shrink sharply between November and March.
