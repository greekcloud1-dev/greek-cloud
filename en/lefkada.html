Ionian · Road-connected

Medical cannabis in Lefkada for visitors

Lefkada is the only Greek island you can drive to. That sounds like trivia, but it is precisely what makes collecting a prescription here simpler than on any other island — there is no ferry timetable between you and a pharmacy.

AirportPreveza / Aktion (PVK), on the mainland
Getting thereSeasonal direct flights, then ~20 km by road
Prescription validity30 days, all of Greece

01 · Access

An island without a ferry

Lefkada is joined to the mainland at the Agia Mavra channel by a floating bridge, in place since 1986, which rotates open on a schedule to let boats pass. Everything else about the island behaves like an island; the arrival does not.

The airport that serves it is Preveza / Aktion (PVK), which is on the mainland. From there to Lefkada Town is roughly 20 km, about 20 to 30 minutes, and the bridge sits only about 1.5 km from the town itself.

Why this matters practically On most islands, if a pharmacy is closed or out of stock, your alternatives are limited by geography. Here the main town is close to the point of arrival and close to the bridge, so a second attempt costs very little.

02 · Where things are

The town, and everywhere else

Pharmacies are concentrated in Lefkada Town, at the northern tip of the island. The resort villages down the east and south coasts — Nidri, Vasiliki and the others — do have services, but the spread is thinner and some reduce their hours outside the season.

The island is long and narrow, so "how far from town" is the question that decides your day. Nidri is a straightforward drive; the southern and western corners take longer, and the west-coast roads above the famous beaches are slow by design.

Plan collection for your arrival day, or for a trip into town. The road in from the airport passes Lefkada Town anyway, so doing it on the way costs almost no detour, which is a much better plan than hunting for an open pharmacy in a resort village at seven in the evening.

03 · At the pharmacy

What happens at the counter

The process is the same everywhere in Greece: the prescription lives in the national e-prescription system, the pharmacist reads it from there, and you are identified by passport: no Greek social security number needed. The passport number on the prescription must match the one you present exactly.

Greek pharmacy opening hours are set regionally, with mandatory opening Monday to Friday only, and a legally binding duty rota covering evenings and weekends that is posted on the front of every closed pharmacy. The detail is on the redemption page, and the official prices — identical at every pharmacy in the country — are on the pricing page.

Call ahead No list of dispensing pharmacies is published by EOF or the Health Ministry, and an open pharmacy is not necessarily one holding stock. On a smaller island that is worth a phone call before the drive.

04 · Season

When to come, and what changes

Lefkada runs a conventional Ionian season. In summer the island is busy and everything is open; in the low season the resort villages contract and Lefkada Town becomes the reliable centre, the same pattern as elsewhere, softened by the fact that the town is easy to reach.

Valid in Greece only The road connection changes nothing legally. The prescription and the product are valid inside Greece, and taking either out of the country is a separate question with a different answer — see flying with cannabis.

05 · Questions

Questions about Lefkada

Which airport serves Lefkada?
Preveza / Aktion (PVK), which is on the mainland rather than on the island. From the airport to Lefkada Town is roughly 20 km, a drive of about 20 to 30 minutes.
Do I need a ferry to reach Lefkada?
No. Lefkada is the only Greek island you can drive to. It is joined to the mainland at the Agia Mavra channel by a floating bridge, which has been in place since 1986 and opens on a schedule to let boats through.
Where are the pharmacies on Lefkada?
Concentrated in Lefkada Town, which sits at the northern tip of the island about 1.5 km from the bridge. Resort villages such as Nidri and Vasiliki have services too, but the spread is thinner and some reduce their hours outside the season.
When is the best time to collect the prescription?
On your arrival day, or during a trip into town. Because the airport road passes Lefkada Town anyway, collecting on the way in costs almost no detour, which is far better than looking for a pharmacy in a resort village at seven in the evening.
Does the road connection change anything legally?
No. The prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in Greece regardless of how you got there, and the product remains valid in Greece only. The road connection is a convenience, not a legal difference.
