Medical Cannabis in Kos — for visitors

Kos is small and flat, and that is a genuine advantage: from anywhere on the island you can reach Kos Town in under an hour, usually much less.

The place

What to know about Kos

  • A compact island — no distances that complicate anything
  • Kos Town concentrates most services
  • Very popular with families
  • Short ferry hops to Rhodes and neighbouring islands

Almost all licensed pharmacies are in Kos Town. The villages — Kardamena, Tigaki, Kefalos — are small, and some have no pharmacy operating outside the season. The drive into town is short, so this is rarely a real obstacle.

Local tipMost flights to Kos are seasonal. Outside the summer months the usual route is via Athens — which conveniently gives you a chance to redeem there instead.

Seasonality

When to come, and what changes

Kos is a summer destination in the clearest sense. Direct flights run mainly between May and October, and outside that window the route goes through Athens. In winter the island is very quiet and a large share of tourist businesses are closed. Kos Town keeps functioning, but at a reduced pace and on shorter hours.

Planning your timing

The island's size is the safety margin here: even if something is not arranged in advance, there is room to fix it. That said, at the height of summer traffic on the coast road is slower than you would expect.

The process

Arriving with a prescription in hand

The process is the same everywhere in Greece, and the prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in the country. You fill in a questionnaire, a Greek-licensed physician reviews it and decides independently, and if approved you receive a digital prescription in your passport name.

You can start before you fly. Full details in the guide, including response times by plan and what happens if the physician does not approve.

What you buy in Greece stays in Greece The prescription and products are valid inside Greece only. Taking cannabis across a border is a criminal offence in most destinations, and a Greek prescription will not protect you at arrival. Details on the flying page.

Questions

What people ask us

We're a family staying in Kardamena. How does this work?
Kardamena is about 25 minutes from Kos Town. The questionnaire and the review happen remotely from your phone, so the only trip required is the redemption itself. Combine it with a day you are going into town anyway.
Can I take the ferry to Rhodes and redeem there?
You can — the prescription is valid throughout Greece and the islands are connected by ferry. But there is no reason to: whatever is available in Rhodes is available in Kos Town.
