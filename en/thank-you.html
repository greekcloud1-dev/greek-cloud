What happens now

The next three steps

  1. The request is in

    Your answers, and the prescription file if you attached one, are stored securely. There is no need to send anything again.

  2. WhatsApp, and the service fee

    We reach out, go through the details and check nothing is missing. The service fee is settled at this point — before the physician reviews the request.

  3. Review by a Greek-licensed physician

    The medical decision rests with them alone, and sending a request is no guarantee of approval. If it is not approved, the fee is returned in full.
