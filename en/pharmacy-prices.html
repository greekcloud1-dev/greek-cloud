Cost at the counter

Medical cannabis prices in Greece

The price is set centrally by the Greek Health Ministry, not by the pharmacy. That means one published figure per product, identical everywhere in the country, and it means the numbers below are checkable rather than an estimate.

02 · Regulation

Why the price is the same at every pharmacy

The Greek Health Ministry publishes a single price bulletin covering all medical cannabis products. Each barcode carries three prices: ex-factory, wholesale and retail. The current order was published on 20 May 2026, under Health Minister decision Δ3(α) 19962/13-5-2026 (ΦΕΚ Β′ 2716).

The retail price is derived from the ex-factory price by a uniform formula: wholesale margin, pharmacy margin, and 6% VAT. Nothing in that chain is set at the counter.

This is good news for you There is no cheaper pharmacy to find. If one pharmacy does not dispense, the next will charge exactly the same amount, so the only thing worth checking ahead is stock, not price.

03 · Oils

Not only flower

Alongside flower there are oral oil solutions, in 10 gram bottles:

  • 3% concentration — €37.27 per bottle
  • 9% concentration — €75.81 per bottle

These are priced per bottle rather than per gram, so they are not directly comparable to the flower figures above. Oils are a relatively late addition to the Greek market.

04 · Reimbursement

Insurance does not cover it

All 24 products in the price order are expressly marked as not reimbursed. The patient pays 100% out of pocket. That is true for Greek residents and for visitors alike, and is unrelated to whatever cover you hold at home.

In practice: the amount in the table above is what you pay at the counter, in full, with no reimbursement from any source.

One cost that is on no price list Inhaled use requires a CE-marked vaporising device notified to EOF. It is bought separately and is not included in the price of the flower. It appears on no cannabis price list, and it surprises people at the counter — budget for it in advance.

05 · Origin

Who makes it, and where

All approved medical cannabis products in Greece come from Tikun Olam, and the strains are its established ones: Erez, Avidekel, Midnight.

The detail that is easy to get wrong: the products are not imported. They are grown and manufactured in Greece itself, at a facility in the Corinth region, under a Greek registration in the name of Tikun Olam Greece SA. Importing medical cannabis into Greece is prohibited, so foreign-grown product could not reach the Greek market in any case.

If you already know these strain names from a scheme in your own country, the genetics will be familiar even though the product and packaging are not the same, which can be useful context when discussing with the physician what has worked for you before.

Prices have fallen, considerably

08 · Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a gram of medical cannabis cost in Greece?
Between €9.46 and €12.85 per gram, depending on the strain and pack size. The figures come from the Greek Health Ministry price order published on 20 May 2026 and include 6% VAT.
Does the price vary between pharmacies in Greece?
No. The price of medical cannabis in Greece is set centrally by Health Ministry order and published officially. The same product costs the same at every pharmacy in the country, so there is no point comparing prices.
Is the 10 gram pack better value than the 5 gram pack?
Yes for most strains. EREVRON, for example, is €11.44 per gram in the 5 gram pack and €10.06 per gram in the 10 gram pack. The exception is the CBD-rich Avidekel strains, where the per-gram price is the same in both pack sizes.
Does health insurance cover medical cannabis in Greece?
No. All 24 products in the price order are expressly marked as not reimbursed. The patient pays 100% out of pocket, and that applies to Greek residents and visitors alike.
Is the vaporiser included in the price?
No. Inhaled use requires a CE-marked vaporising device notified to EOF. It is purchased separately, is not included in the price of the flower, and does not appear on any cannabis price list — budget for it in advance.
