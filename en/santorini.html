Medical Cannabis in Santorini — for visitors

Santorini is a small island built around luxury tourism. The infrastructure exists, but it is limited — and this is exactly where landing with a prescription already in hand is worth a day of your holiday.

The place

What to know about Santorini

  • Fira and Oia concentrate the services
  • Short distances, but heavy traffic in season
  • A very popular honeymoon destination
  • Regular ferries to Crete and Mykonos

Pharmacies are concentrated in Fira, the island's capital. Oia and the other villages are far more limited. At peak season traffic on the main road is very slow, and a drive that should take 20 minutes can take an hour.

Local tipThe typical stay in Santorini is three or four nights. On that schedule, starting a medical process on the island is simply wasted effort — start before you fly.

Seasonality

When to come, and what changes

Santorini has the sharpest seasonality of any Greek island. Between May and September it is packed, prices peak and traffic is heavy. In winter many hotels and restaurants in Oia close entirely and Fira is the only fully functioning centre. There are few direct international flights — the usual route is via Athens by domestic flight or ferry.

Planning your timing

With three or four nights and a physician review window measured in days, the arithmetic does not work unless you start early. This is the destination where advance planning stops being a convenience and becomes the requirement.

The process

Arriving with a prescription in hand

The process is the same everywhere in Greece, and the prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in the country. You fill in a questionnaire, a Greek-licensed physician reviews it and decides independently, and if approved you receive a digital prescription in your passport name.

You can start before you fly. Full details in the guide, including response times by plan and what happens if the physician does not approve.

What you buy in Greece stays in Greece The prescription and products are valid inside Greece only. Taking cannabis across a border is a criminal offence in most destinations, and a Greek prescription will not protect you at arrival. Details on the flying page.

Questions

What people ask us

We only have two days in Santorini. Is it realistic?
Only if the prescription is already in hand. With two days on the island, any process that begins after you land will finish after you have left. Start a week before and land ready.
We're staying in Oia. Do we need to go to Fira?
Most likely yes. Fira concentrates most services on the island, pharmacies included. The drive is short at normal times, but around sunset — when everyone is heading to Oia — it can be very slow. Plan for the morning.
