The short answer

How does a visitor get a medical cannabis prescription in Greece?

You approach a Greek-licensed physician while you are in Greece. The physician reviews your case and decides independently whether medical cannabis is appropriate. If it is, they issue a digital prescription you redeem at a licensed pharmacy. A licence or prescription from another country is not part of this and carries no weight in Greece. GreekCloud coordinates the request, translates and guides — nothing more.

Legal framework Regulated since 2017 Pharmacy dispensing began in February 2024, overseen by the National Organisation for Medicines (EOF).

What to know before you go further The decision rests with the physician alone. Under Greek law, medical cannabis is considered where conventional treatments have not helped, were unsuitable or were not tolerated — so submitting a request is no guarantee of approval. Eligibility

I do not have a serious illness. Is there any point?

Possibly. These are conditions physicians in Greece do assess in practice — not an eligibility list, not a criterion, and not a promise.

  • Chronic pain and persistent back pain
  • Sleep disorders and insomnia
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Migraines and recurring headaches
  • Muscle and joint pain
  • Appetite and digestive problems
  • Inflammatory conditions
  • Other conditions, at the physician's discretion
The decision is medical and rests with a Greek-licensed physician, based on an individual assessment and subject to Greek law. Meeting any item above does not guarantee approval. If the physician does not approve, the service fee is refunded in full.

FAQ

What people ask before they start

Is medical cannabis legal in Greece?
Yes. Legislation regulating medical cannabis has been in place since 2017, and pharmacies began dispensing in February 2024. Use without a prescription remains an offence, and recreational cannabis is not legal in Greece. Oversight sits with the National Organisation for Medicines (EOF).
Do I need a medical cannabis licence from my own country?
No. The process is governed by Greek law alone. The Greek physician performs a fresh, independent assessment. People who have never held a licence anywhere can still be assessed.
Can I bring my own medical cannabis into Greece?
No. A foreign licence or prescription is not valid in Greece, and bringing cannabis in without prior authorisation from the Greek medicines authority is prohibited — with exposure beginning at customs. A notarised translation of a foreign document is not an authorisation and confers no status.
How long does it take?
The questionnaire takes about three minutes. The physician's response time depends on your plan: about an hour during working hours on VIP, within 24 hours on Fast, and one to two weeks on Basic. Starting before you fly is strongly recommended.
What if the physician does not approve?
The service fee is charged when you submit, before the physician decides. If they do not approve, the full amount is refunded with no cancellation charge.
How much does the product itself cost?
The pharmacy sets that price and it varies by product, concentration and location. It is not within our control and is not included in the service fee. You pay the pharmacy directly.
Who decides the strength and quantity?
The physician alone, based on your situation and subject to Greek regulation. It is not a patient choice. The range available in pharmacies changes over time and between pharmacies.
What happens to my medical information?
It is used solely to pass your request to the physician and have it reviewed. It is not sold and not shared with third parties for marketing. Full details in the privacy policy.
Are you doctors?
No. GreekCloud is an administrative coordination service. We do not issue prescriptions, we are not a pharmacy, we do not sell or transport products, and we do not give medical advice. Every medical decision is made by a Greek-licensed physician.
