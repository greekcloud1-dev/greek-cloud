Borders and customs

Flying cannabis into Greece or out of it: the short answer is no

This is the page most people reach hoping to find a loophole. There isn't one — and it is worth understanding exactly why, because the mistake here ends in a criminal file, not a fine.

01 · Inbound

Flying into Greece with your own medical cannabis

Prohibited. Including with a valid medical cannabis licence from home, and with a signed prescription.

Bringing cannabis into Greece requires prior authorisation from the Greek medicines authority. Without it, this is the import of a controlled substance — and the exposure starts at customs in the Greek airport, before you have had a chance to explain anything to anyone.

Why a licence from home offers no protection

Medicines regulation is territorial. A licence issued under your own country's law governs what you may do there. It creates no standing under Greek law, and a Greek customs officer is not bound by it.

What people tell themselves "It's a small amount", "I have a doctor's note", "nobody checks". The first two are legally irrelevant, and the third is a gamble where the cost of losing is wildly out of proportion to what you save.

02 · Outbound

Taking home what you bought in Greece

Prohibited, and this is the more serious of the two. Importing cannabis without a licence is a criminal offence in most countries.

The Greek prescription — even if entirely valid, in your passport name, and inside the 30 days — does not protect you at your arrival airport. It is a Greek document whose force ends at the Greek border.

This includes remnants. A bag with a gram left in it, an empty package with residue, a product forgotten in a coat pocket — all of it counts. If you bought it in Greece, finish it in Greece.

The simple rule worth remembering What is bought in Greece stays in Greece. Plan the quantity around the length of your stay, not around what you would like to take home.

03 · The document that does not work

Why a notarised translation is not an authorisation

This is one of the worst pieces of advice circulating online, and it sounds convincing — so it is worth taking apart.

What a notary does: certifies that a translation is faithful to the original. That is all. They verify the quality of the translation, not the validity of the underlying document.

What a notary does not do: confer standing on a document in another country, turn a foreign licence into a Greek authorisation, or oblige any foreign authority to recognise it.

The result: a foreign licence, translated and notarised, is exactly the same foreign licence — only in two languages. It does not make the import legal, and it will not change anything at customs.

The same logic applies to an apostille. An apostille authenticates the signature of the issuing authority; it does not make the content valid across a border.

04 · Common question

What about flights within Europe?

Even between EU member states, medical cannabis does not move freely. There is a European arrangement allowing patients to carry controlled medicines with a specific certificate issued by the country they are departing from — but it applies to residents of member states, under conditions, and for limited periods.

For visitors from outside the EU this is not relevant. There is no route in which a non-EU document permits carrying cannabis into or around Europe.

The conclusion is the same in every direction: the only lawful way to obtain medical cannabis while in Greece is a local prescription, redeemed and consumed there.

05 · The alternative

What you can do instead

Approach a Greek-licensed physician, before your flight or during your stay, and obtain a local prescription redeemed at a licensed pharmacy in Greece. That route is lawful, regulated, and requires you to carry nothing across any border.

  • No licence from home is needed, and no translated documents.
  • You can start before you fly and land with the prescription ready.
  • Valid at any licensed pharmacy in Greece, for 30 days.
  • If the physician does not approve, the service fee is refunded in full.
